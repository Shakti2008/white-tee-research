# Evidence standards

1. Use official product pages for specifications and current prices. Record date, region, currency, variant, and stock state.
2. Attribute marketing statements as brand claims. A sustainability claim is not independent verification.
3. Separate observation, inference, hypothesis, and unknown. Never fill unknown specifications from intuition.
4. Compare list price and promotional price separately. Divide pack price by garment count; record shipping and tax treatment separately.
5. Use customer reviews as qualitative evidence with platform, date, sample size, and selection limitations. Do not infer prevalence from a few anecdotes.
6. Link every material factual claim to a source and retain an evidence ID. Recheck prices and policies before using them in decisions.
7. Verify physical performance with a consistent sample protocol. Marketing copy cannot establish opacity, shrinkage, collar durability, or feel.

## Status definitions

- **Queued:** lead only; relevance unverified.
- **Starter:** initial source observations; major fields missing.
- **Desk research complete:** product, policies, positioning, and sources captured.
- **Sample tested:** physical observations recorded with method.

## Sample protocol draft

Record size, measurements before wash, lighting for opacity checks, wash/dry instructions followed, measurements after repeated wash cycles, collar shape, twisting, pilling, and evaluator notes. Keep purchase and batch dates. Decide the final protocol before comparing samples.
