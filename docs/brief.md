# Project brief

## Objective

Understand the competitive landscape for a future clothing brand built around a plain white T-shirt. For now, collect and organize evidence; product development and launch planning come later.

## Research scope

- Core product: plain white T-shirt; record off-white separately.
- Core lens: brands built around one product or a tightly focused product category.
- Include brands that began with a hero product, while recording their current assortment accurately.
- Include broader apparel brands only as explicit price, product, or customer experience benchmarks.

## Decisions still open

| Decision | Current status | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer and gender/fit range | Undecided | Interviews and existing buying behavior |
| Initial sales geography | Undecided | Demand, logistics, returns, and competitor coverage |
| Price tier | Undecided | Comparable unit prices and willingness to pay |
| Fabric, weight, cut, opacity | Undecided | Product specifications and physical samples |
| Primary differentiation | Undecided | Repeated customer needs and competitor delivery |

Use US/USD as an initial comparison convention only; this is not a market selection decision. Preserve original currencies and never silently convert them.
