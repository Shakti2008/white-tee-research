# Son of a Tailor

**Status:** Starter · **Reviewed:** 2026-11-09 · **Group:** Fit-led apparel benchmark

## Evidence captured

The homepage presents custom fit and made-to-order clothing, with production at its own SON Supply facility and local partners. Current merchandising includes polos and knitwear as well as T-shirts. The homepage advertises a free remake if the buyer is dissatisfied with fit within two months of purchase; full eligibility terms still need review. [SOAT-01: Homepage](https://www.sonofatailor.com/)

## Research interpretation

A useful comparison for fit as a buying proposition and for reducing fit uncertainty. The visible assortment is broader than a single T-shirt product. These are research interpretations, not verified evidence of conversion or customer preference.
